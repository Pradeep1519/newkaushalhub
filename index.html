Common questions

Do I need prior AI experience?

No. Foundation programmes are designed for beginners. Technical programmes include their own recommended audience and prerequisites.

Can a programme be customized for our campus?

Yes. Institutions can request a tailored curriculum, duration, delivery mode and cohort plan through the institutional enquiry form.

How do I confirm a student registration?

Submit your registration details to receive a unique ID. Payment is not collected on this website yet.
